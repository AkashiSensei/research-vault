## 2026-08

- [[paper-vault/papers/radford2018improving]]
  - Title: Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training
  - Use in paper: Unlabeled pre-training corpus of more than 7,000 unpublished books spanning multiple genres, selected for long contiguous text.
  - Why listed here: Supplies the document-level language signal from which GPT learns transferable causal representations before supervised fine-tuning.
