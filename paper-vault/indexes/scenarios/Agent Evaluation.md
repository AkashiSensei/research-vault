# Agent Evaluation

## 2026-07

- [[paper-vault/papers/wang2024autonomousagents]]
  - Title: A survey on large language model based autonomous agents
  - Scenario focus: Evaluating LLM agents with subjective judgments, objective metrics, protocols, and benchmarks.
  - Why listed here: Supplies a taxonomy for asking whether an agent is effective, efficient, and behaviorally plausible across different evaluation settings.

## 2026-09

- [[paper-vault/papers/yehudai2026evaluation]]
  - Title: A Survey on Evaluation of LLM-based Agents
  - Scenario focus: Building a layered evaluation program for interactive agents and diagnosing their trajectories.
  - Why listed here: Connects task benchmarks to benchmark-design dimensions and development frameworks, making it useful for both test selection and failure analysis.
- [[paper-vault/papers/mohammadi2025evaluation]]
  - Title: Evaluation and Benchmarking of LLM Agents: A Survey
  - Scenario focus: Specifying evaluation coverage from offline testing through dynamic simulation and production monitoring.
  - Why listed here: Offers a what-versus-how checklist that includes repeated-run reliability, cost, safety, tooling, and deployment context.
- [[paper-vault/papers/zhu2027evolutionary]]
  - Title: Evolutionary perspectives on the evaluation of LLM-based AI agents: a comprehensive survey
  - Scenario focus: Matching an agent's environment and capabilities to current and future benchmark coverage.
  - Why listed here: Provides reference tables and a two-stage procedure for selecting tests that fit today's system and its likely evolution.
