# Decoder-Only Language Models

## 2026-08

- [[paper-vault/papers/radford2018improving]]
  - Title: Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training
  - Topic focus: Causally masked Transformer decoder used as a reusable language representation model.
  - Why listed here: Establishes the decoder-only GPT backbone and shows that the same pretrained stack can be fine-tuned across several language-understanding interfaces.
- [[paper-vault/papers/radford2019language]]
  - Title: Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners
  - Topic focus: Larger decoder-only language models trained on a broad web corpus.
  - Why listed here: Preserves the causal GPT architecture while scaling model capacity, context, and training data to study emerging zero-shot task behavior.
