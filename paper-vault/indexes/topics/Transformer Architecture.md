# Transformer Architecture

## 2026-08

- [[paper-vault/papers/vaswani2017attention]]
  - Title: Attention Is All You Need
  - Topic focus: Attention-only encoder-decoder architecture for sequence transduction.
  - Why listed here: Defines stacked encoder and decoder blocks built from multi-head attention, position-wise feed-forward layers, residual pathways, normalization, and positional encoding.
- [[paper-vault/papers/devlin2019bert]]
  - Title: BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding
  - Topic focus: Encoder-only Transformer adapted for deeply bidirectional language representation.
  - Why listed here: Retains the Transformer encoder while changing its input representation and pretraining objectives so one backbone can transfer across language-understanding tasks.
