# Literature Survey

## 2026-07

- [[paper-vault/papers/wang2024autonomousagents]]
  - Title: A survey on large language model based autonomous agents
  - Role in paper: Evidence-synthesis method for mapping the early LLM-agent field.
  - Mechanism: Curates representative studies and organizes them into architecture, capability, application, and evaluation taxonomies.
  - Why listed here: Functions as a field map and shared reading framework rather than proposing one new agent design.

## 2026-09

- [[paper-vault/papers/yehudai2026evaluation]]
  - Title: A Survey on Evaluation of LLM-based Agents
  - Role in paper: Structured review method for constructing a focused map of LLM-agent evaluation.
  - Mechanism: Searches four academic sources, follows citations, applies explicit inclusion and exclusion rules, and consults domain experts before organizing the selected works.
  - Why listed here: Supplies unusually concrete review-method detail for this fast-moving topic, while stopping short of a PRISMA-style systematic-review audit trail.
- [[paper-vault/papers/mohammadi2025evaluation]]
  - Title: Evaluation and Benchmarking of LLM Agents: A Survey
  - Role in paper: Narrative synthesis supporting an objective-versus-process evaluation taxonomy.
  - Mechanism: Curates benchmark, metric, and tooling examples and groups them by evaluation target, procedure, and enterprise concern.
  - Why listed here: Provides a compact field framework, but does not disclose a reproducible literature search or screening protocol.
- [[paper-vault/papers/zhu2027evolutionary]]
  - Title: Evolutionary perspectives on the evaluation of LLM-based AI agents: a comprehensive survey
  - Role in paper: Narrative benchmark review supporting environment, capability, trend, and selection taxonomies.
  - Mechanism: Compares published benchmark attributes in extensive tables and synthesizes directional changes across four evaluation modules.
  - Why listed here: Offers broad benchmark coverage and actionable organization, but does not report how the underlying literature corpus was found or screened.
