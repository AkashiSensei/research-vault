# Agent Evaluation Taxonomy

## 2026-09

- [[paper-vault/papers/yehudai2026evaluation]]
  - Title: A Survey on Evaluation of LLM-based Agents
  - Role in paper: Organizing capability, domain, generalist, benchmark, and framework views of evaluation.
  - Mechanism: Layers the evaluation target and then profiles benchmark construction and assessment granularity.
  - Why listed here: Provides the broadest of the three maps for designing a multi-level agent evaluation program.
- [[paper-vault/papers/mohammadi2025evaluation]]
  - Title: Evaluation and Benchmarking of LLM Agents: A Survey
  - Role in paper: Separating evaluation objectives from evaluation processes.
  - Mechanism: Crosses behavior, capability, reliability, and safety with interaction, data, scoring, tooling, and context choices.
  - Why listed here: Converts a fragmented landscape into a compact operational checklist, especially for enterprise systems.
- [[paper-vault/papers/zhu2027evolutionary]]
  - Title: Evolutionary perspectives on the evaluation of LLM-based AI agents: a comprehensive survey
  - Role in paper: Classifying benchmark families by environment and capability.
  - Mechanism: Places each benchmark in an external-world category and an internal-competence category, then records comparison attributes.
  - Why listed here: Makes benchmark discovery sensitive to both where an agent acts and which competence is being tested.
